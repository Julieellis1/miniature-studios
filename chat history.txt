I want to start creating tiktok content using AI video generator to create miniature life. Suggest types of videos i can create

Absolutely. Miniature Life can become a strong TikTok concept because AI video is especially good at making ordinary Nigerian life look absurdly tiny, cinematic, and funny.

The key is to build repeatable video series, rather than making random miniature clips.

1. Tiny Nigerians Doing Normal Nigerian Things

Take everyday situations and shrink the people.

Tiny mechanics repairing a giant car
Tiny people cooking jollof rice in a giant pot
Miniature street traders selling food
Tiny electricians fixing a huge ceiling fan
Miniature barbers cutting hair
Tiny cleaners washing a giant Lagos danfo
Tiny construction workers building a house
Tiny people queuing at a giant ATM
Tiny delivery riders delivering a package to a gigantic house

Hook: “POV: You entered the smallest Lagos in the world…”

2. Miniature Lagos

Make Lagos itself the character.

Ideas:

A miniature Lagos traffic jam
Tiny danfo buses fighting for road space
Miniature LASTMA officers directing giant cars
Tiny market sellers competing for customers
Miniature street food vendors
Tiny people surviving Lagos rain
A miniature version of a Lagos bus stop
Tiny workers repairing a pothole that's enormous to them
A tiny Lagos neighborhood waking up at 5 AM

You could make this an ongoing universe: “Mini Lagos.”

3. Giant Object, Tiny Workers

This could become one of your signature formats.

Imagine a normal household object becoming an enormous construction site.

Examples:

50 tiny workers repairing a giant smartphone
Tiny engineers repairing a giant refrigerator
Miniature mechanics fixing a giant generator
Tiny cleaners washing a giant sneaker
Tiny construction workers building a giant plate of food
Tiny farmers harvesting grains from a giant bowl of rice
Tiny firefighters trying to extinguish a candle

The visual joke is immediately understandable without dialogue.

4. “How It's Made” — But Ridiculous

Pretend ordinary things are manufactured by miniature workers.

Examples:

“How Nigerian Jollof Is Made”

Tiny workers:

Harvest tomatoes
Transport them in tiny trucks
Crush them
Build a giant cooking station
Add rice
Panic when someone says the food is burning 😂

Other episodes:

How a phone battery is made
How a barber haircut is made
How a football is manufactured
How a Nigerian wedding is organized
How a plate of suya is made
5. Miniature Comedy Skits

Instead of relying entirely on visual spectacle, give your mini characters personalities.

For example:

Character A: hardworking miniature delivery guy.

Character B: lazy coworker.

Character C: their extremely serious boss.

Each episode is 20–40 seconds.

Example:

Boss: “Where is the delivery?”
Worker: “I'm almost there.”
Camera reveals he's standing at the bottom of a gigantic staircase.

That gives you recurring characters people can recognize.

6. Miniature Horror

This could work extremely well because the scale difference creates tension.

Tiny people trapped inside a giant house
Miniature workers discovering a giant mysterious footprint
Tiny people exploring an abandoned refrigerator
Tiny characters hearing footsteps approaching
Tiny security guards protecting a giant building
A miniature neighborhood where something enormous appears every night

Keep it suspenseful rather than graphic.

7. Miniature Animals + Humans

Make animals enormous from the miniature characters' perspective.

Tiny farmer confronting a gigantic chicken
Tiny mechanic repairing a giant cat's collar
Miniature people building a house inside a dog's kennel
Tiny workers rescuing a kitten
Miniature fishermen catching a gigantic fish
Tiny security guards protecting a giant egg

This gives you lots of opportunities for cute + funny content.

8. Miniature Football Universe

Given your previous commercial concepts, this could fit your style particularly well.

Imagine:

“Inside a Football Match”

Tiny workers operate a giant football stadium:

Miniature groundskeepers maintaining the pitch
Tiny technicians operating the scoreboard
Tiny vendors selling food
Tiny security guards chasing a streaker
Tiny cameramen filming the match
Tiny fans celebrating a goal
Tiny medics running onto the pitch

You could even create recurring miniature football characters.

9. “One Giant Thing”

Make one ordinary object the entire environment.

For example:

Episode 1 — The Giant Phone

A miniature city exists inside someone's phone.

Episode 2 — The Giant Shoe

Tiny workers manufacture and clean the shoe.

Episode 3 — The Giant Plate

Miniature farmers grow food on it.

Episode 4 — The Giant Wallet

Tiny security guards protect someone's money.

This gives the channel a recognizable visual identity.

10. Miniature “POV” Videos

These are particularly TikTok-friendly.

POV: You're 3 inches tall in Lagos.

You walk through:

a kitchen
a market
a bus station
a restaurant
a football stadium
a construction site
a Nigerian wedding

The camera stays at miniature eye level, making normal objects look enormous.

11. Tiny People vs. Everyday Problems

This is probably one of the easiest formats to produce consistently.

“When you're tiny but…”

Your phone falls off the table
Your food is on a giant plate
Someone spills water
The AC turns on
Your mother calls you
NEPA takes the light
It starts raining
Your bus arrives
Someone drops a bottle nearby

The comedy comes from treating an ordinary event like a disaster movie.

12. Miniature Jobs

Every episode explores an absurd miniature profession.

Examples:

“A Day in the Life of a Tiny Plumber”
“A Day in the Life of a Tiny Uber Driver”
“A Day in the Life of a Tiny Nigerian Chef”
“A Day in the Life of a Tiny Security Guard”
“A Day in the Life of a Tiny Football Coach”
“A Day in the Life of a Tiny Delivery Man”

This could become a very recognizable series.

A particularly strong direction for your channel

I'd build Miniature Life around three recurring pillars:

Series	Content
🏙️ Mini Lagos	Tiny Nigerians navigating giant versions of everyday Lagos
😂 Tiny Problems	Tiny characters dealing with ordinary problems that become enormous
🔧 Tiny Workers	Miniature workers doing impossible jobs

Then occasionally add Miniature Horror, Miniature Football, and Miniature Animals for variety.

Example 7-day posting cycle

Monday: Mini Lagos — “A tiny man tries to cross Lagos traffic.”

Tuesday: Tiny Workers — “How 100 tiny workers clean a giant sneaker.”

Wednesday: Tiny Problems — “When you're 3 inches tall and someone drops a bottle.”

Thursday: Miniature Football — “The tiny stadium workers preparing for match day.”

Friday: Mini Lagos — “A tiny delivery man gets his biggest delivery yet.”

Saturday: Miniature Horror — “Something enormous has entered the tiny neighborhood.”

Sunday: Miniature Comedy — “Tiny landlord comes to collect rent.”

The important part is character consistency. Instead of generating completely different people every time, create 3–5 recognizable recurring characters with fixed clothing, faces, personalities, and roles. Then your audience starts following the characters, not just the AI visuals.

And since you're using AI video, I'd make most videos 15–35 seconds, with a strong visual hook in the first 1–2 seconds and a simple story/payoff at the end.
